TROVE: a new app (launching 1 November) to discover and book events and experiences in Johannesburg and Pretoria: comedy shows, events, sip-and-paint, skydiving and more.
Browse what's on, book in under a minute, pay by card or Instant EFT, and get a QR ticket by email (free) or on WhatsApp (R5 extra). Show the QR at the door.
Ticket prices are set by each host (for example a comedy show around R150, sip-and-paint around R350, a tandem skydive around R3,000). TROVE adds an R8 booking fee to every booking. It is not refunded if you cancel, but you get everything back if the host cancels.
Your payment is held by TradeSafe (escrow) and only paid to the host after the event. Each event shows its refund policy before you pay.
