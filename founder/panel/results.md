## What would flip a no

- If a show I already want to see on Instagram linked straight to TROVE and I could book there without installing anything, with the WhatsApp ticket included free.
- If TROVE had good experiences I genuinely can't find on my usual apps, like a curated weekend list of sip-and-paint or adventure stuff around Sandton that I'd otherwise have to hunt for on Instagram.
- If it had an event my friends wanted that I couldn't get on my usual apps, or if one of the group used it first and said it was smooth, with no booking fee on the first booking.
- If one of the guys drops a TROVE link in the group chat for something good happening tonight that we can't book anywhere else, and I can book all eight of us in a minute without making an account.
- If the venues I follow on Instagram put a TROVE link in their bio, or it let me book and split for my whole group of eight in one go, I'd use it.
- An event I actually want to go to that is only listed on TROVE, plus reviews from friends showing that refunds really came through.
- Real reviews and photos from past attendees on each event page, plus friends or reviewers I trust saying they booked through TROVE without problems.
- If a date-night thing I saw on Instagram could only be booked through TROVE, or the fee was dropped for a first booking so I could try it once.
- A friend from my WhatsApp group booking through it first and getting a refund or entry with no problems, or the hosts I already trust listing it on their own pages as an official way to book.
- If I open it at 7pm and it shows what's on tonight near Pretoria East that I can still book, especially things that sell out or don't take door sales, I'd use it.
- Friends using it with no problems, or a first booking with no booking fee, for an event I already wanted to go to that's cheaper or only available on TROVE.
- No booking fee, or an event that sells out and that I can only get through TROVE, or a real discount on the ticket that covers more than the fee.

Buyers say they would buy **1.3 times** in the first month on average.
